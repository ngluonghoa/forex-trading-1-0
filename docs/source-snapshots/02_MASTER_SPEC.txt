PHẠM VI Ở THỜI ĐIỂM KHỞI TẠO (LỊCH SỬ): phân tích kỹ thuật Forex, LONG/SHORT, watch/early/confirmed, source-quality audit và Backtest. Trạng thái đặc tả hiện hành đọc §0/§21/§22; tuyệt đối không coi đoạn khởi tạo này là nguồn chuẩn mới nhất.
1. MỤC TIÊU NGHIỆP VỤ VÀ PHẠM VI
Input: một hoặc nhiều cặp tỷ giá, market/as-of timestamp, source/broker assumptions, optional position and account/risk constraints.
Output: cho mỗi cặp hướng LONG và SHORT riêng, vùng chờ/mức kích hoạt/early/confirmed, Entry/SL/TP1/TP2, R:R net of modeled costs, invalidation, main/secondary setup, time horizon, Market/Session/Macro Context, 8-step evidence, 8 audit topics, source-quality, cache stats, NO TRADE/ERROR/INCOMPLETE.
Giới hạn P1: major/cross currency pairs, không tự gộp vàng XAUUSD, bạc, crypto, chỉ số CFDs dưới cùng thị trường Forex; các loại đó đòi instrument class riêng. Không đặt lệnh. Nguồn chỉ được đọc theo quyền truy cập.
Phân biệt INFO_SIGNAL (mô hình phát hiện), TRADE_ELIGIBLE (đủ nguồn/chi phí/risk), ORDER_PLACED (ngoài phạm vi) và HISTORICAL_VALIDATED (kiểm nghiệm thực tế). Cấm gắn nhãn trading-eligible khi thiếu nguồn/clock/stop/costs cần thiết.
2. MÔ HÌNH TRẠNG THÁI — PHẢI TÁCH HAI TRỤC
DIRECTION = LONG | SHORT.
STRATEGY_HORIZON = FAST | SWING (tên, tối thiểu/tối đa holding duration chỉ là dự thảo; không lấy cổ phiếu SHORT 3–10 phiên, SWING 10–60 phiên làm default).
SETUP_PHASE = WATCH_CANDIDATE | APPROACHING | EARLY | CONFIRMED | INVALIDATED.
SIGNAL_STATUS = WAIT_EARLY | WAIT | EARLY_SIGNAL | CONFIRMED_SIGNAL | NO_TRADE | UNVERIFIED | ERROR. Giao diện tiếng Việt có thể hiện CHỜ MUA SỚM / CHỜ MUA / MUA SỚM / MUA XÁC NHẬN và đối xứng bên bán, nhưng không biến nhãn hiển thị thành lệnh được khớp.
POSITION_STATUS = NOT_PROVIDED | FLAT_VERIFIED | OPEN_VERIFIED | STALE | CONFLICTING; đồng thời biết direction, units và margin when provided. Nếu không có position, không được suy ra tài khoản flat.
ZONE_ACTION = ENTRY_LONG | ENTRY_SHORT | EXIT_LONG | EXIT_SHORT | WATCH_ONLY; ZONE_AUXILIARY có thể gắn cảnh báo chống mua đuổi/chốt lời độc lập với lệnh SHORT.
RULE_EVALUATION = DETECTED | NOT_DETECTED | UNVERIFIED | NOT_APPLICABLE (chỉ dùng N/A khi rule thực sự ngoài strategy/instrument scope, có reason). Danh mục 16 vùng là lịch sử kế thừa. Danh mục hiện hành gồm 18 nhãn theo §16 và được quản lý động theo phiên bản; không khóa cứng số lượng.
3. 8 BƯỚC PIPELINE DỰ THẢO — GIỮ CHUẨN MINH BẠCH
01 DATA MANAGER: pair registry, provider/broker scope, source license, timeframe price-side, source revision, L1 cache/source delta.
02 DATA VALIDATOR: timestamp/provider time, timezone/DST, weekend/rollover, bid/ask OHLC, stale/crossed quotes, spread anomalies, missing bar, tick-volume provenance and source history completeness.
03 ANALYSIS ENGINE: trend W1/D1/H4/H1/M30/M15 + conditional M5; structure/supply-demand/ranges, currency strength and cross-pair regime, volatility/session/news; indicators with versioned dependencies.
04 POSITION & EXIT PROTECTION: verified open positions, adverse spread/stop-loss, margin/free margin/stop-out, expected swap/rollover, weekend/gap and news exposure; no assumed active position.
05 ZONE DETECTION: all active catalog rules with separate LONG/SHORT/EXIT applicability, main/secondary zone IDs, watch, early and confirmed evidence.
06 SIGNAL ENGINE: strategy_horizon × direction, Entry/SL/TP1/TP2/max chase, net R:R, pip/lot sizing, correlated currency risk, invalidation and expiry.
07 AUDIT & DECISION: eight independent audits, hard fail, source/price-side/calendar/news/portfolio feasibility, ranking, NO TRADE allowed.
08 REPORT & HISTORY: atomic versioned evidence+snapshot, verified status, stable UI template, no contradictory FULL_REPORT when critical source missing; store event IDs for audit and replay.
Mỗi stage có NOT_STARTED/RUNNING/COMPLETE/FAILED/BLOCKED với timestamp và evidence; cache re-use không đồng nghĩa stage COMPLETE nếu input không được tái xác minh. No 8/8 verified claim from mocked data.
4. HỢP ĐỒNG DỮ LIỆU FX v0.1 — CANDIDATE
InstrumentKey = {pair, base_ccy, quote_ccy, instrument_kind, broker_or_reference_market, precision, pip_size, tick_size, contract_size, quote_currency, account_currency_conversion_spec_version}.
ProviderObservation = {provider_id, source_stream_id, endpoint, raw_snapshot_digest, quote_side, first_known_utc, retrieved_at_utc, license_scope, source_revision, timezone_source_policy, broker_contract_version}.
MarketBarKey = {instrument_version, timeframe, bar_start_utc, bar_end_utc, source_calendar_version, aggregation_policy_version, quote_side}; separate from provider observation identity and revision. BID, ASK and MID are semantically different bar inputs, not silently interchangeable.
Quote = {event_time_utc, first_known_time_utc, bid, ask, source_id, stale_flags, quote_spread, quote_revision}; require bid<=ask and nonnegative prices unless specific type contradicts and has handling. MID=(bid+ask)/2 when both available; MID is a derived reference, not executable. OHLC for BID/ASK should derive from quote streams where possible; snapshot OHLC MID alone is insufficient to certify executable fills.
Bar quality axes independent: lifecycle OPEN/CLOSED; coverage COMPLETE/PARTIAL/SPARSE_UNVERIFIED/UNKNOWN/INVALID; source quality VERIFIED/WARN/UNVERIFIED/BLOCKED. A CLOSED bar with bad source is NOT a confirmed signal.
Time labels: source event timestamp, observation first-known timestamp, scan decision timestamp, candle close cutoff timezone-aware; reject mismatched parsing and ambiguous DST. W1/D1 period policy can be broker/source-specific, especially New York close and Sunday short candles.
Tick volume/quote count: provider-specific proxy only, not aggregate global market volume. Reject default 'no quote = no trade' inference; neither volume=0 nor empty candles implies zero currency market turnover.
Source conflict: if two providers/BID/ASK/origin revisions disagree, mark conflict; never silently merge, even when all digest SHA256 match.
Data retention: raw source logs may contain restricted/licensed content. Record permission before persistence or GitHub snapshot; no secrets/raw market feeds committed.
5. PHÂN TÍCH, CHỈ BÁO VÀ NGỮ CẢNH
Mandatory to evaluate context: multi-TF trend and structure; price action; key levels/ranges; volatility; session context; spread/execution risk; available macro events; pair and portfolio exposure when multiple pairs.
Candidate indicators: EMA/SMA, RSI, MACD, ADX/DMI, ATR, Bollinger/Keltner, ROC, Fibonacci/Pivots; choose only when rule-specific improvement survives validation.
Conditional/research: Wyckoff, VSA, VCP, AVWAP/VWAP, OBV, MFI, RVOL-TOD, order-flow/footprint, ICT/SMC, ML. Data-dependent indicators must use valid proxy or UNVERIFIED; not all required.
Currency Strength must handle base/quote signs and pair coverage; derived dollar strength and EURUSD cannot be treated as independent confirmations if driven by same prices.
MacroEvent: scheduled_release_time and actual_release_first_known_time distinct; no later actual values used to predict past decisions. Event severity/blackout thresholds are candidates, not assumed fixed.

7. FOREX RISK/EXECUTION PRINCIPLES
LONG buy enters at ASK, closes at BID; SHORT enters at BID, closes at ASK, subject to broker/product specifics. Stop/TP activation and fill must be simulated with the executable side, not against midpoint only.
Net PnL = direction-aware entry/exit difference × units, converted to account currency using timestamped conversion prices, minus actual/slippage/commission/spread already embedded in bid/ask and financing/swap costs as applicable. Avoid double-counting spread if using bid/ask fills and adding fees.
Risk needs equity/account currency, pip value conversion, stop distance, units/lot increments, margin requirement, maximum leverage, drawdown/stress, weekend gaps and multiple correlated positions. Unknowns -> cannot claim exact position size.
Trailing stop/Stop loss not guaranteed to execute at exact price in gap/illiquid conditions. Model nonfills/partial fills and stop-outs if product supports.
Spread/rollover/news/counterparty are hard risk blockers by per-strategy thresholds to be calibrated, not arbitrarily auto-approved.
8. CACHE / EARLY DETECTION
L1 raw/normalized multi-side quotes/bars, session events, macro, source quality, currency registry and broker contract.
L2 indicators, currency strength, relative/correlation metrics; independent price-side/TF/source-version hashes.
L3 structure/pivots/BOS/CHoCH, range and breakout event history, causal first-known clocks.
L4 zone phases, LONG/SHORT ideas and risk input dependencies; no repeated alert on rescanning same closed bar.
L5 audit, report snapshots, manifest, versioned signal history; immutable old evidence with supersedes.
M15 always analyzed when verified; M5 triggered conditionally by near-zone or risk-protection and not by repeated polling. A provisional M5 can display watch/preview only. Clock-based expiry requires defined calendar/rollover policy; correction replays prior M15 state, not counts as another new bar.
Refresh every ~5 minutes on demand should reuse historical snapshots and recompute only changed windows; server-side delta must be verified rather than promised.
9. BACKTEST & SIGNAL VALIDATION ENGINE
Backtest must use exactly the same detector/risk implementation as analysis engine. Separate ReplayClock, HistoricalMarketDataPort, TradeSimulator, ExperimentManifest, EventScorer, PortfolioSimulator and Metrics; no code paths for live orders.
Layer 1 EVENT METRICS: precision/recall with defined label horizon, false alerts per pair-session, early lead time and lost moves; label outcomes never leak into predictor inputs.
Layer 2 TRADING SIMULATION: bid/ask cost after first-known time, spread variation, commission/financing, margin leverage, account conversion, weekend gaps, news events, unfilled/partial fills, portfolio currency concentration. Net expectancy in R, fill rate, drawdown, profit factor and uncertainty, not win rate alone.
Layer 3 ROBUSTNESS: chronological train/validation/walk-forward/untouched OOS, regime stratification by session/volatility/event, provider sensitivity, multiple comparisons and survivorship/availability. Final untouched OOS is not used to tune.
Start with synthetic correctness harness and one complete executable detector; FIRST REAL historical backtest only with licensed time-appropriate data and complete execution assumptions. Do NOT wait all 16 detectors, but do NOT skip required data gates.
Compare baseline: M30 only vs M15 vs M15+adaptive M5, using same data scope/fee assumptions. H4 enhancement should be tested as independent ablation, not assumed beneficial.
